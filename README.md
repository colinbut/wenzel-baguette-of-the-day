# Wenzel's Baguette of the Day

## Sypnosis

Wenzel's UK's nationwide known bakery chain has this daily offer where they offer a 'Baguette of the Day' for only £2.50 whereas the original price of the baguette comes at a much higher price of around £4-5 or there abouts. 

My wife loves Wenzel's baguette of the day offering because it is such a good deal to bag a baguette for a decent cheap price. Not that she's a baguette lover or anything.

So I created this app for my wife. It is to enable her to open up this app on any given day to see what the day's 'Baguette of the Day' deal is without having her to leave the comfort of her own home to go into a store specifically to check out.

This is due to the fact of inconviency of Wenzel not publishing their Baguette of the Day schedule before hand. 

Fret not!

After weeks and weeks of constant daily data gathering I have managed to figure out their schedule and thus built this into an app for my wife to use. :)

## Developing

Once you've created a project and installed dependencies with `npm install` (or `pnpm install` or `yarn`), start a development server:

